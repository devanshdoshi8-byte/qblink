- One account may hold several roles (customer + business); guards check `roles.includes(...)` and sign-up adds a role instead of blocking. Why: users were locked out of business mode after first signing in as a customer.
- Pre-OAuth intent (role/next/business draft) lives in `src/lib/auth/pendingAuth.ts` (localStorage + TTL). Why: sessionStorage was lost on phones that return from Google in another tab.
- Signed-in queue joins use join_customer_queue; customer profile reads and leaving use owner-scoped RPCs keyed to auth.uid(), never phone matching or client-supplied identity. Why: cross-device visit history must not expose or claim another customer's visits.
- Customer visit updates subscribe to PII-free queue_live_signals with polling and online refresh fallback, not raw queue_visitors realtime. Why: keep customer identity out of public update streams.
- Business analytics use queueAnalytics helpers for local calendar-date trends and hourly buckets. Why: production calculations and regression tests must share logic without collapsing weeks or omitting hours.
- Health Coach requests use healthAIRequest with a current authenticated session token and no anonymous fallback. Why: the protected endpoint requires a user identity.
- Back controls use explicit in-app parent destinations rather than browser history; leaving a queue page never removes a token. Why: QR entries and OAuth tabs may have unrelated or missing history.
- Customer-detail saves update queues scoped by business_id, while other integrity settings remain scoped to the counter family. Why: existing QR links must share the detail requirement without changing unrelated queue behavior.
- Waiting-ticket language and advisory return buffers use shared waitingExperience helpers; turn sound and screen wake lock are owned by the join page. Why: the alert must survive the waiting-to-called view transition without changing queue priority or asserting doctor activity.
- Keep ROI assumptions and calculations locally typed and render the calculator with JSX rather than React.createElement aliases; this preserves contextual event typing without suppressing checks.
- Keep the MCP edge function manually authored and out of the Vite codegen plugin; runtime type annotations must survive Deno validation.
- Always cross-check the existing codebase (components, routes, migrations, and demo pages) before claiming a feature is missing or proposing a new implementation. Why: Qblink already contains mechanisms for duplicate prevention, interactive live demos, and waiting buffers; proposing existing features causes confusion and wastes development effort.
